## How a round works

- The host chooses how many dots each person gets (from 1 to 20, 5 to start with) and chooses **Start voting**.
- A strip under the top bar then says "Voting is open" and how many of your dots you have left. Screen readers hear when voting starts, when you've placed your last dot, and when it ends.
- Put your dots on the notes you like best, as many on one note as you want, until they run out. Select a note and use **Add a dot** or **Remove a dot** under it, or in **Properties**. On a phone, tap the note and use **Votes** in its editor. With the keyboard, press **D** on a focused note to add a dot and **Shift+D** to take one off.
- When the host chooses **Stop and reveal**, voting ends for everyone. Each note shows its total, and the notes with the most dots say **Top voted** (several if they're tied).
- **Results** lists the notes from most dots to fewest. Choose one to go to that note. On a wider screen the list is in **Properties** with nothing selected; on a phone, choose **Show results** on the strip. If nobody voted, it says "No votes were cast."

## Private and anonymous

- Only you see your dots while voting is open. Nobody else sees them, the host included, and nobody sees any totals until the host stops the round.
- Nobody sees who voted for what, ever: the results are only totals.
- Your dots belong to this browser on this device, so they're still there after a reload or in a second tab. Clearing this browser's site data resets them: you'd vote again as someone new, and your earlier dots stay counted.
- A round can have at most 40 voters. If it's full, the vote buttons say "This vote is full."

## Rounds, the lock and deleted notes

- Starting a new round clears the previous round's dots and results. **Clear votes** removes every vote and turns voting off.
- Voting still works while the board is locked: everyone can place dots even though they can't change the notes.
- If a note is deleted, any dots on it come back to the people who placed them.
- Undo and Redo never change votes.

The host's controls are described in [Running a session](help:hosting).
