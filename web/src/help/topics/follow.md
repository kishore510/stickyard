## Follow someone

- In **Participants**, choose **Follow** beside someone's name. Your view then goes where they look on the board, at their zoom, as they move around. It works on a phone too.
- A strip under the top bar says **Following Sam**, with **Stop**. Until their view arrives it says "Waiting for Sam's view." Phones and hidden tabs don't share a view, so following someone on a phone, or someone who has switched to another tab, shows nothing until they're back on a wider screen's board.
- Following stops when you move the view yourself: dragging the board, the mouse wheel, pinching, the zoom keys, **Fit to notes**, **Zoom to selection**, **Go to**, or the overview map. **Stop** (or **Stop following** in **Participants**) stops it too. The board then says "Stopped following Sam."
- Following someone else replaces it. If the person you follow leaves, the board says "Sam left." Up to 10 people can follow one person at a time; past that, the board says "Sam already has 10 people following." and you follow nobody.
- Following ends when either of you reconnects or leaves; follow again from **Participants**.

## When people follow you

- On a wider screen a small count, for example "2 following you", shows under the top bar while anyone follows you. It never says who.
- While anyone follows you, the point at the centre of your view and your zoom are passed on to them, at most five times a second and only when your view moves. Phones never pass theirs on. None of it is stored. See About > Privacy.

## Bring to me (host)

- The host can ask everyone to come to their view: **Session** in the top bar, then **Bring to me** (on a phone, **Session** in **Participants**).
- Everyone else gets a banner, "Sam asked everyone to come to their view", with **Go there** and **Dismiss**. Nobody is moved until they choose **Go there**. **Esc** dismisses it too. A newer one replaces it, and it goes by itself after a minute; the host can always send it again.
- If you're following someone, the banner says **Go there** stops that, and it does.
- After the host brings everyone, the button is off for five seconds and says "Wait a few seconds." It works on a locked board and during a silent brainstorm: it sends only where the host is looking, never any notes.
- Nothing is held back for people who join later.

## Screen readers and motion

- Screen readers hear when following starts, stops or ends, and when the host asks everyone to come to their view, never each move.
- The view moves briefly to the person you follow and then keeps up with them directly; with reduced motion it never animates.
