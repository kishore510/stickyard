Chat sends short messages to everyone in the session, with your name.

- On a phone, open **Chat** from the button at the top or from the menu. On a wider screen, it's the round button at the bottom right of the board.
- While chat is closed, a dot on its button means there are messages you haven't seen. It clears when you open chat.
- Type in **Message** and choose **Send** (or press **Enter**). Messages can be up to 280 characters, and the newest is shown first.
- Each message shows when it arrived on your device (with the date too if it wasn't today). Point at the time to see the full date.
- On a wider screen, make chat bigger or smaller by dragging the grip at its top left. With a keyboard, move to the grip with **Tab** and use the **arrow keys** (**Shift** for bigger steps). Double-click the grip to go back to the default size. This browser remembers the size.
- Messages are plain text: formatting and links are shown exactly as typed.
- Messages aren't saved. Someone who joins later doesn't see earlier messages, and they're gone when you leave or reload.
