## Placing a stencil

- On a wider screen, the palette has a **Stencils** tab beside **Add**. It holds built-in stencils (a labelled area with three blank notes, a three-step flow, a note cluster in a group, a decision, and pros and cons) and the ones you've saved.
- Click a stencil to place it in the middle of the view, or drag it onto the board. Everything in it is added at once (notes, shapes, frames, then its arrows and groups), and one **Undo** removes all of it.
- If the board hasn't room for everything in a stencil (notes, shapes, frames, 100 arrows or 30 groups), nothing is added and the page says what's missing.
- When the host locks the board, guests can't place stencils. During a silent brainstorm nobody can. The tiles say why.

## Saving your own

- Select some notes, shapes and frames (arrows and groups come along), then choose **Save as stencil** in the top bar (in **Arrange** on a narrower screen) or in **Properties**, and give it a name of up to 40 characters.
- A stencil keeps the selected items with their sizes, colours, styles and text, the arrows between them, and the groups whose items are all selected. An arrow attached to something you didn't select keeps a free end where it was.
- It includes the text of the selected items, and it's saved in this browser only: nothing is sent to anyone until you place it on a board. It isn't shared with other people or your other browsers.
- Up to 20 stencils, each at most 24 KB. In the Stencils tab, **Rename** and **Delete** sit beside each saved stencil, and **Delete all** removes them all.
- If this browser blocks storage (some private modes do), the tab says so: saving won't work, and the built-in stencils still do.
- A saved stencil that can't be read (changed by hand, say) is left out, and the tab says how many.
- On a phone there's no Stencils tab.
