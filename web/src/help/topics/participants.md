In a session, the **Participants** button at the top opens a panel. It's also in the menu.

- On a wider screen, the button shows the people in the session as small round faces with their initials, in their colour: you first, then up to two more, then **+N** for the rest. On a phone it's a people icon with a count.
- A short message at the top of the board says who joined or left. If several people arrive or leave together, one message says how many. There's none for you, for the people already there when you join, or for someone who drops out and comes straight back.

- It lists everyone in the session now, each with a coloured dot and their name. Your own name says **(you)**, and the session's host says **Host**. See [Running a session](help:hosting).
- If you're the host, it also has a **Session** section on a phone, with the timer, **Lock board** and **End session**.
- A session holds up to 20 people at once.
- Names aren't verified: anyone with the link can join and type any name. See [Names and identity](help:names).
- **Copy link** copies the session's link, to share with the people you want to invite.
- **Leave session** takes you back to the start page. Your notes stay on the board.

## Cursors

- On the board you see other people's pointers as small arrows in their colour, with their name beside them. They move as the person moves their mouse over the board, fade after a few seconds without movement, and disappear when the pointer leaves the board or the person leaves.
- Pointers are passed on live and never stored: nobody can see where anyone's pointer was earlier.
- Yours is shared while your mouse (or a pen hovering over the screen) is over the board, on a wider screen, and only while someone else is in the session.
- Phones show other people's cursors, but they don't share one. Neither does a touch screen, or a pen pressing.
- In the **Participants** panel, under **Cursors**: turn off **Show other people's cursors** if you find them distracting, or turn off **Share my cursor** to stop others seeing yours. This browser remembers both.
