## Reacting to a note

- Select a note and choose **Add reaction** (the smiley with a plus) under it, then pick one of six: **Thumbs up**, **Heart**, **Laugh**, **Celebrate**, **Thinking** or **Plus one**. The picker closes once you've chosen.
- Each reaction a note has shows under it as a small chip with the emoji and how many people chose it. Yours have a thicker outline and a tinted fill.
- Choose a chip to add your reaction of that kind, or to take yours off. You can give a note several kinds, but each kind only once.
- On a phone, tap the note: its editor has the same **Reactions** row at the top, with **Add reaction**, and the picker opens from the bottom of the screen. On a wider screen the same row is in **Properties**.
- Choosing a chip or **Add reaction** never selects, moves or edits the note.

## Who sees what

- Everyone sees how many of each reaction a note has. Nobody sees who reacted, the host included; only your own pages know which reactions are yours.
- Your reactions belong to this browser on this device (the same as your dots in [Dot voting](help:dot-voting)), so they're still yours after a reload or in a second tab.
- Reactions work in any session, with or without a vote, and on a locked board. Starting a new voting round or clearing votes doesn't touch them.
- During a [silent brainstorm](help:silent-brainstorm), hidden notes have no reactions and no **Add reaction**, not even for their writer. Once the host reveals them, they work like any other note.
- A reaction goes when its note is deleted, when the board is cleared, and with the session.

## Zooming out

- Zoomed out below 50%, a note's chips turn into one small summary (a few emoji and the total) so the board stays readable; below 25% they're hidden. Zoom in to choose them again, or use the note's editor or **Properties**.
- An exported PNG shows the counts as they are on screen, but never which reactions are yours. The Markdown export doesn't include reactions.

## Limits and messages

- You can have up to 100 reactions in a session, and a board can hold 2,000. When a reaction can't be saved, its chip goes back and a short message says why (for example "You’ve used all your reactions (100). Remove one to add another.").
- While you're not connected, a reaction isn't saved; you'll see "You’re not connected, so your reaction wasn’t saved."

## Keyboard and screen readers

- **Tab** reaches the chips and **Add reaction** under the selected note; **Enter** or **Space** chooses one. Each chip is read like "Thumbs up, 3, you reacted. Press to remove."
- In the picker, **Tab** moves between the six emoji and stays inside it; **Esc** closes it and focus goes back to **Add reaction**.
- Screen readers hear your own reactions ("You reacted with Heart."), never other people's. A note's name includes its reactions, for example "Reactions: Thumbs up 3, Heart 1."
- With reduced motion, chips don't pop when you react.
