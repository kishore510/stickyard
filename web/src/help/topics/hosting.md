## Who the host is

- Whoever starts a session is its host. Stickyard remembers that on the device the session was started from, so you're host again whenever you rejoin from there.
- In this version host powers can't move to another device: if you open the session somewhere else, or clear this browser's site data, you join as an ordinary participant there.
- Hosts are marked: **Participants** says **Host** next to their name, and on a wider screen their face in the top bar has a small crown.

## Timer

- Everyone sees the timer in the top bar while one is running: minutes and seconds (hours too for long ones). It goes by the relay's clock, so it's the same for everyone, and it's right again as soon as you come back to a tab you'd left.
- In the last minute it says **Last minute**; at the end it says **Time's up**. Screen readers hear when it starts, when 1 minute is left, and when time's up. Nothing plays a sound.
- **Time's up** stays until the host stops or replaces the timer, or for 10 minutes.
- The host starts one on a wider screen from **Timer** under **Facilitation** in the palette, and on a phone from **Session** in the **Participants** panel. Pick 1, 3, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, or type any number of minutes from 1 second to 3 hours (2.5 is two and a half minutes). Starting one while another runs replaces it.
- The host can **Restart** or **Stop** it from the timer itself (on a phone, from **Session**). Stop asks first when more than a minute is left.

## Locking the board

- The host can **Lock board** (on a wider screen at the right of the board buttons in the top bar; on a phone in **Session**). It waits for the relay to confirm, then says **Locked**.
- While it's locked, everyone else sees "The host has locked the board. You can still chat and look around." Adding, moving, editing, deleting, arranging, undo and clearing are greyed out and say "The board is locked by the host." Hosts keep editing.
- **Unlock board** gives everyone their editing back, and the board says so.

## Dot voting

- The host runs dot voting from **Session** at the right of the board buttons in the top bar (choose **Voting**), or on a phone from **Session** in **Participants**.
- Set **Dots each** (1 to 20) and choose **Start voting**. While a round is open or its results are showing, the button says **Start a new round**. It asks first, because a new round clears the last one.
- **Stop and reveal** asks "End voting and show results to everyone?" and then shows everyone the totals. **Clear votes** asks first, then removes every vote and turns voting off.
- The host votes like everyone else, and sees only totals too: not who voted, or how many people have voted so far.
- These controls aren't available while you're not connected, or while something is still being added or the board is being cleared.
- See [Dot voting](help:dot-voting) for what everyone else sees.

## Ending a session

- The host can **End session** (on a wider screen in **Properties** with nothing selected, next to **Clear board**; on a phone in **Session**). It asks once: the board is deleted for everyone and can't be restored.
- Everyone, the host included, then sees **Session ended**, and the session's link stops working.
- It isn't available while you're not connected, or while something is still being added or the board is being cleared.
