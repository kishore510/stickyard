## What it is

- A silent brainstorm lets everyone write ideas without seeing anyone else's first, so nobody anchors on the first idea or the loudest voice.
- The host chooses **Start silent round**. From then on, every note added stays hidden from everyone but the person who wrote it, until the host chooses **Reveal notes**. Then everyone sees all of them at once.
- Notes that were on the board before the round started stay where everyone can see them.

## Who sees what

- While the round runs, you see the notes everyone could see before, plus your own new notes. Each of yours says **Only you** above it, and **Properties** (or the editor on a phone) says "Only you can see this note until the host reveals the notes."
- Nobody else sees your hidden notes, not their text, colour, place or size: **the host can't see them either**.
- Everyone sees how many notes have been written: a strip under the top bar says "Silent brainstorm: write your ideas. Nobody sees them until the host reveals.", how many you've written and how many there are in total. **Properties** with nothing selected counts the notes you can see and the hidden ones.
- You can edit, move, resize, restyle and delete your hidden notes as usual, and undo works. Each person can write up to 40 notes in a round; the strip says how many you have left when you're close.
- Other people's pointers still move during a round, so someone may see roughly where you're working. If you'd rather not show yours, switch off **Share my cursor** in **Participants**.

## Tied to this browser

- Your hidden notes belong to this browser on this device. A reload or a second tab in the same browser shows them again.
- Another device, another browser, or this browser after its site data is cleared can't see your hidden notes until the reveal; there, you're a new writer.
- If this browser is blocking storage, the page can't take part in the round: you can still watch, and you'll see everything at the reveal.

## The reveal

- Only the host's device can reveal. **Reveal notes** asks first, says how many notes are hidden, and that it can't be undone. Everyone then sees every note at once, and the strip goes. Screen readers hear how many notes were revealed.
- Your view doesn't move. If some of the new notes are out of view, the board says "Some revealed notes are out of view." with **Fit to notes**.
- If the host loses their device during a round, nobody can reveal: the hidden notes stay hidden until the session expires (after 7 days with nobody in it). Ending the session deletes them with everything else.

## While it runs

- Frames can't be moved (their grip looks greyed out and says why), though they can still be resized, renamed and recoloured.
- **Clear board**, **Start voting** and **Export** are off and say "Silent brainstorm is on." or "Export is off during a silent round."
- If voting was already open, it stays open: you can vote on the notes everyone can see, but not on hidden ones until they're revealed.
- A locked board still stops guests adding notes, as usual.

The host's controls are described in [Running a session](help:hosting).
