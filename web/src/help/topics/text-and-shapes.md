Besides notes and frames, a board can hold **shapes**: a **Text** label, a **Rectangle**, an **Oval** or a **Diamond**, each with text inside. Shapes sit among the notes: which one is in front is shared with notes, and frames are always behind both.

## Adding a shape

- On a wider screen, find **Text**, **Rectangle**, **Oval** and **Diamond** under **Shapes** in the palette. Click one to add it in the middle of the part of the board you can see, or drag it onto the board to drop it where you let go. Its text is ready to type.
- A **Text** label has no fill and no border, so only its words show. While it's empty it has a faint dashed outline and the word "Text" in grey, so you can find it; that word is never saved.
- A board holds up to 50 shapes and text labels. When it's full, the tiles are switched off with the reason.
- On a phone, shapes are shown, with their styles, but can't be added or changed.

## Typing in a shape

- **Double-click** a shape, or select it and press **Enter**, to type in it. **Enter** starts a new line; **Esc**, or clicking elsewhere, saves the text. A shape holds up to 500 characters.
- Text wraps inside the shape and anything that doesn't fit is cut off at its edge; make the shape bigger to see it all. In an oval or a diamond the text stays inside the outline.
- The smiley button beside the shape (and beside **Text** in Properties) adds an emoji where you're typing. Each counts as one character.
- If the connection drops while you're typing, what you typed stays (read only) and is saved when you're back.

## Styling it

Select one shape and **Properties** shows:

- **Text**: the words, saved when you leave the field.
- **Text style**: **Size** (Small, Medium, Large and Extra large, then three heading sizes), **Bold**, **Italic** and **Underline**, **Align** (left, centre or right), **Vertical** (top, middle or bottom) and **Text colour**. Every text colour stays readable on every fill, and on the board, in light and dark.
- **Shape** (not for a Text label): **Fill** (none, or one of seven colours), **Border colour**, **Border width** (none, thin, medium or thick) and **Border style** (solid or dashed).
- **Size**: **Width** (40 to 2400) and **Height** (24 to 1600). Or drag a corner of the selected shape; **Alt** and the arrow keys resize it from the keyboard.
- **Order**: **Bring to front** and **Send to back**, the same as for notes.

With two or more shapes selected (and nothing else), **Text style**, **Fill** and **Border** change all of them at once; **Text** shows **Mixed** when they differ and is edited one shape at a time, and **Width** and **Height** are off (use **Match size** under **Arrange**).

## With notes and frames

- Select shapes as you select notes: click, **Shift**-click or **Ctrl**-click (**Cmd**-click on a Mac), a marquee (it picks up every shape it touches), or **Ctrl+A** for everything.
- Drag any selected item to move the whole selection, or use the arrow keys. A frame brings the shapes inside it (any shape whose middle is inside) as well as its notes; **Alt** leaves them behind. A frame holding more than 50 notes and shapes together moves on its own.
- **Align**, **Distribute**, **Grid** and **Match size** work on notes and shapes together. With frames in the selection as well, they're off, and the Arrange panel says why.
- **Duplicate** (**Ctrl+D**) copies shapes with everything they have, and **Delete** asks once for a mix, with how many of each. One **Undo** brings back what was deleted.
- If two people change the same shape, the change that reaches the relay last wins.
