## Drawing an arrow

- On a wider screen, the palette has a **Connectors** section: **Arrow**, **Line** and **Elbow arrow**. Choose one, then on the board either drag from where the arrow starts to where it ends, or click the start and then click the end. **Esc** cancels.
- An end near a note or shape snaps to it: its four connection points show (the middle of each side), and the end attaches when you let go. Near a side's middle it attaches to that side; elsewhere on the note it attaches to whichever side faces the other end. Anywhere else, the end stays where you put it.
- Select a note or shape and drag one of the four dots beside its sides to draw an arrow straight from that side.
- A session holds up to 100 arrows.

## Arrows that follow

- An attached arrow follows its note or shape when anyone moves it, resizes it, arranges it, or moves the frame it's in.
- Deleting a note or shape keeps its arrows: their ends stay where it was. Before you delete one with arrows attached, the confirmation says how many will keep a free end. **Undo** puts the note back and attaches the arrows to it again, unless someone else changed them meanwhile.
- Arrows sit above frames and below notes and shapes. An arrow that isn't attached at all doesn't move with a frame.

## Changing arrows

- Click an arrow's line to select it; **Shift** or **Ctrl** adds it to the selection. A selection box takes the arrows with both ends inside it. **Ctrl+A** selects arrows too.
- Drag a selected arrow's round end handles to move an end, free it from its note, or attach it somewhere else. An arrow with both ends free can be dragged by its line; an attached one moves with its notes instead (drag one of its ends).
- **Properties** shows one or several selected arrows: **Route** (straight or elbow), **Dash** (solid, dashed or dotted), **Width**, **Ink**, a head at the start or the end, and **Reverse** (swaps the ends). With nothing selected, Properties counts the arrows.
- **Delete** removes selected arrows. **Duplicate** copies arrows whose ends are on copied notes and shapes, or free; an arrow attached to something outside the selection isn't copied.
- **Clear board** removes arrows too, and one **Undo** brings everything back.
- With the keyboard, **Tab** reaches each arrow (its name says what it joins), **Delete** deletes it and **Esc** clears the selection.

## When you can't change them

- On a phone, arrows are shown but can't be drawn or changed.
- When the host locks the board, guests can't change arrows. During a silent brainstorm nobody can, the host included. The controls say why.
- Export: the PNG shows the arrows; the Markdown file lists them under **Connections** as "A → B", with "(free end)" for an end that isn't attached.
