## Making a group

- On a wider screen, select two or more notes or shapes and press **G**, or choose **Group** in the top bar. A tinted dashed box appears round them with a label chip at its top-left. Frames and arrows can't be in a group: if they're selected too, they're left out and the page says so.
- A note or shape is in one group at most. Grouping items that are already in another group moves them into the new one.
- A group holds 2 to 100 notes and shapes, and a session holds up to 30 groups. A group left with fewer than 2 items (taken out or deleted) is ungrouped by itself; its items stay where they are.

## Working with a group

- Click the chip, or the box's outline, to select the group: its items are selected and **Properties** shows the group. Clicks inside the box still reach the notes and the board.
- Drag the chip to move every item in the group together. Moving one item on its own never takes it out of the group: the box follows it.
- Double-click the chip (or select it and press **Enter**) to type a label, up to 40 characters; **Enter** saves it and **Esc** cancels. You can also change the label in **Properties**.
- **Properties** for a group: **Label**, **Colour** (it tints the box only, never the notes), how many members it has, **Add selection** (Shift-click or Ctrl-click other items first), **Remove selection** (Shift-click or Ctrl-click to leave only the ones to take out), **Select members** and **Ungroup**. **Shift+G** ungroups too. Ungrouping keeps every item.
- With nothing selected, **Properties** counts the groups.
- Deleting items asks first, and says how many groups would be left with fewer than 2 items and ungrouped. **Undo** brings the items back into their group.
- **Duplicate** copies a group only when all of its items are selected: the copy holds the copies. **Clear board** removes groups too, and one **Undo** brings everything back, groups included.

## Affinity grouping

Groups make affinity grouping quick, and nothing is grouped for you:

1. Everyone writes ideas on notes (a [silent brainstorm](help:silent-brainstorm) works well), then the host reveals them.
2. Drag notes that belong together into clusters.
3. Select a cluster, press **G**, and name it. Repeat for each theme.
4. Use [dot voting](help:dot-voting) on the notes, or export the board: the Markdown file lists each group under its label.

## When you can't change them

- On a phone, groups and their labels are shown, but can't be changed.
- When the host locks the board, guests can't change groups. During a silent brainstorm nobody can, the host included. The controls stay in place and say why.
- At low zoom the label is shortened, and below 25% only the outline shows.
- Export: the PNG shows the boxes and labels; the Markdown file lists each group as a sub-list under its label, in the section of the frame its box sits in.
