## Reconnect and presence (v0.13.0)
- Web only, no protocol or stored-schema change. Reconnect lives in `RoomSession` (same session, so the board, chat and viewport stay): pure rules in `connection/reconnect.ts` (`backoffDelay` 1 s doubling to `RECONNECT_MAX_DELAY_MS` 30 s, ±20% jitter; `RECONNECT_MAX_ATTEMPTS` 8 then phase `offline`; `PROBE_AFTER_FAILED_OPENS` 5 (3 before v0.15.0) sockets that never opened → `checkHealth` (`probeHealth` in `connectionCheck.ts`, GET /health) then `checkCode` (/rooms/check); health down → phase `limit`: one probe every `LIMIT_RETRY_MS` 60 s while visible, at most `LIMIT_MAX_PROBES` 60, no sockets; `HIDDEN_GRACE_MS` 60 s then tries wait for visible; event-started tries `MIN_TRY_GAP_MS` apart). `ConnectionEnv` (online/hidden/listen) is injected: `browserConnectionEnv` in `useRoom`, `STATIC_ENV` by default; `random` too.
- `RoomView.reconnect` `{ phase: reconnecting | network | offline | full | limit, attempt, max }` (null while joined). Status stays `disconnected` throughout; fatal outcomes keep their old states (`reload` for version mismatch or a newer relay, `invalid` for a link the relay refuses); `room_full` on a reconnect is phase `full` (no auto retry, Rejoin). `online` restarts the sequence (not for `full`); `offline` while joined drops at once (phase `network`). `rejoin()` restarts now; it uses `storedName` (`stickyard:name`), else the name it joined with. Each socket's events are tagged (`socketGen`): a replaced socket's late events are ignored.
- The drop (`dropped`): counts my unconfirmed work with `unsavedKeys` (pending adds/deletes, held items, `History.pendingKeys()`; items of a template, restore, delete or clear run are left to their own reports), then `discardUnconfirmed` (board back to confirmed; drafts kept), one `dropReport` (`DROP_TEXT.unsaved(n)`, cleared by the next note action). Queued itemsAdd, clear frames, abandoned sets and the history all go; runs end partial as before. A draft on a note that goes becomes `orphanDraft` (`restoreDraft()` adds it back as a new note, `dismissDraft()`).
- Join key (v0.27.0): `join` carries the page's per-room key (the voter key, `stickyard:voter:<room id>`, `voterKey()`) on the first join and every reconnect, so before any snapshot; it names my writer for silent brainstorm. Never shown or logged; without one (no storage) the page joins without it and can view, but can't add notes during a silent round (`RoomView.writer` false). During a round the join step ends with `silentMine` (facilitation.md).
- Resync: on a reconnect `joined` updates you/participants but the board is live only at the `snapshot`, which replaces every note (`resyncNotes`, drafts kept by id); the following `framesSnapshot` replaces every frame (`resyncFrames`). No merge by rev; `synced` stays true (no refit). The history is cleared on the drop and on every snapshot. `RoomView.yourIds` holds every participant id of this visit (`authorName`, `countUnread` use it).
- Inline editing survives a drop read-only (`InlineText readOnly`), then commits normally; `uiStore.pruneSelected`/`pruneFrame` also drop an inline edit or edit request whose item is gone. `History.nextExpiry()` drives a session timer so Undo/Redo reasons refresh when a waiting change is given up on (the 0.12 stale-button bug).
- UI: `ConnectionBar` (top of the board's notice stack, `data-connection-status`, `role=status`, text from `rooms/connectionText.ts`; the limit state says "may be"), `data-drop-report`, `data-orphan-draft`. Tests: `test/reconnect.test.ts`, `test/reconnectSession.test.ts` (fake relay: `drop`, `failOpen`, `open`, `holdFrames`, `joinError`), UI in `roomsUi.test.tsx`.
- Presence: `presence/avatars.ts` (`avatarStack`: you first, `AVATAR_MAX` 4 places, the last is `+N` when more; `initials`, `truncateName` (`NAME_MAX_SHOWN` 16), `peopleLabel` for the button's accessible name) and `presence/AvatarStack.tsx` (md and up, in the Participants button; ring = `participantBorderClass`, size `--sy-avatar`); phones keep the count button. Toasts: `presence/toasts.ts` `summarizePresence` (a leave then a join of the same name, or a join then a leave of the same id, cancel out); the session batches (`TOAST_BATCH_MS` 1 s, a leave waits `LEAVE_GRACE_MS` 3 s, toasts `TOAST_GAP_MS` 2 s apart, shown `TOAST_SHOW_MS` 5 s) into `RoomView.presenceToast` (replaces `announcement`). No toast for yourself, the joined list, names back within `RESYNC_QUIET_MS` 15 s of your own reconnect or of their shown leave, or a leave while the same name is still here (a quick join still cancels a waiting leave). Rendered in the board's notice stack in an always-present `aria-live="polite"` region (`data-presence-toasts`), no buttons, `sy-fade-in`.

## Live cursors, protocol v14 (v0.19.0)
- `PROTOCOL_VERSION = 14`; v13 pages get `version_mismatch`. Client (strict): `cursor { x, y }` (finite numbers within the board ± `CURSOR_TOLERANCE` 64) and `cursorLeft {}`. Server (strict): `cursorMoved { id, x, y }` (whole units, `clampCursor` to the board) and `cursorGone { id }`. The id is the sender's participant id from its socket; name and colour come from the participant list. Both client types are `BOARD_WRITES: false` (a locked board still shows cursors). No stored-schema change.
- Relay (`room.ts` `cursor`): parsed before any rate budget; a valid cursor message spends `CURSOR_LIMITS` (limits.ts: 15/s, burst 20) and never `SOCKET_LIMITS` (malformed ones spend SOCKET_LIMITS and get bad_message). Over budget: dropped silently; past `maxSilentDrops` (100) in `dropWindowMs` (10 s) every further drop is a violation (`violation(..., null)`: counted, no reply). Forwarded at once to the other joined sockets: no coalescing, no timer, zero storage calls (tested by wrapping every storage method; `Room.timersPending` for the no-timer check). Before joining or alone: nothing sent. The attachment holds the bucket, drops and `cursorShown`; `hideCursor` sends `cursorGone` before `participant_left` (`leave`: close, error, rate-limit close) and for every shown cursor before `sessionEnded`. Costs in docs/LIMITS.md.
- Web pure rules: `cursors/cursors.ts` (`CURSOR_SEND_INTERVAL_MS` 100, `CURSOR_MIN_MOVE` 1, `CURSOR_IDLE_MS` 5000, `CURSOR_LABEL_CHARS` 12 = `--sy-cursor-label-max`; `cursorDecision`, `CursorSender` (leading + one trailing send, clock and timers injected), `pointerMaySend` (mouse, or a pen with no buttons: by pointer type, never screen size; touch never), `onBoardPoint` (off the board = leave), the cursor map (`moveCursor`, `removeCursor`, `idleCursors`, `nextIdleAt`), `placeCursor` (inside the visible area, scale 1/zoom, label flip), `cursorLabel`, `CursorSink`).
- Session: `shareCursor(x, y)` (only live, visible and with someone else here; rounded; straight to the socket, no view update or history), `hideCursor()` (cursorLeft once if shown; also on hiding the tab). `cursorMoved`/`cursorGone` are handled before the view machinery (never a view update) and go to `options.cursors`; my own ids and unknown participants are ignored; `participant_left` removes; drop, close, roomGone, every `joined` and a resync `snapshot` clear.
- Stores: `cursors/cursorStore.ts` (`useCursors`, `cursorSink`; one idle timer, nothing ticks while nothing moves); `cursors/prefs.ts` (`useCursorPrefs` show/share, `STORAGE_KEYS.showCursors` / `shareCursor`, "on"/"off", written only on change).
- Page: `cursors/useCursorSharing.ts` in `BoardCanvas` (md and up: pointermove → `screenToFlowPosition`; pointerleave, window blur, hidden tab and Share off → leave). `cursors/CursorLayer.tsx` inside `<ReactFlow>` via `ViewportPortal` (`data-cursor-layer`, aria-hidden, pointer-events none, `.sy-cursor-layer` z above every note); one memoised `CursorMark` per id reads only its own entry, so notes never re-render (render-count test mocks `NoteNode`). Inline SVG arrow: `--sy-cursor-outline` under `--sy-cursor-halo` (one of them 3:1 on every note, frame header, board and canvas colour, both themes, tested), fill = participant colour; label `bg-participant-N` with `--sy-cursor-label-fg` (4.5:1, tested). `--sy-duration-cursor` / `--sy-cursor-fade` are 0 under reduced motion. Phones draw with `data-compact` (smaller arrow and label) and never send.
- Participants sheet: `CursorSection` with two `role=switch` checkboxes (Show other people’s cursors, Share my cursor). Help: Participants topic, Cursors section. Privacy: pointers live, never stored; the switches stored here.
- Tests: `shared/test/cursors.test.ts`, `worker/test/cursors.test.ts`, `web/test/cursors.test.ts`, `web/test/cursorsSession.test.ts` (includes a two-page relay), `web/test/cursorsUi.test.tsx`.

## Navigation: last known pointers (v0.24.0)
- Web only. `CursorSink` gains `left(id)`: the session calls it on `participant_left` (was `gone`). `cursors.ts` `LastPositions`, `rememberPosition`, `forgetPosition` (pure). `cursorStore` keeps `lastSeen` beside `cursors`: `moved` updates both; `gone` (cursorGone) and the idle fade touch only `cursors`; `left` removes both; `clear` (every `joined`, drop, close, resync `snapshot`) empties both, so a reconnect forgets every position. Never stored; tracked whatever the Show switch says (the switch only hides `CursorLayer`). Phones receive pointers, so Go to works there.
- Participants (`rooms/ParticipantsPage.tsx`, the same component as the phone sheet and the md panel): each other person's row has `GoTo` (`data-goto`, label "Go to <name, 16 chars>’s pointer", `aria-disabled` with a visible `JUMP_HINT` reason via `aria-describedby` until `lastSeen` has them). A press closes the sheet (`closeSheets`) and calls `uiStore.requestJump`; the board does the rest (canvas.md "Navigation"). Tests: `test/lastSeen.test.ts` (fade, cursorGone, leave, reconnect, Show off, no `setItem`), `test/navigationUi.test.tsx`.


## Follow and Bring to me, protocol v19 (v0.30.0, part 1 of 2)
- Protocol, relay and web plumbing only; no visible UI (part 2). `PROTOCOL_VERSION = 19`; v18 pages get `version_mismatch` ("please reload"). No stored-schema change: nothing is stored and nothing is scheduled. Bring to me (host only) is in facilitation.md.
- A view on the wire is the board point at the centre of someone's canvas and their zoom (`{ x, y, zoom }`), so it means the same on any screen size; `follow.ts viewFromCentre` makes one from React Flow's viewport and canvas size. Bounds in shared: x and y within the board ± `VIEWPORT_MARGIN` 256 (the canvas pans `PAN_MARGIN` = a note's width past each edge, so a centre is always inside), zoom within `MIN_ZOOM` 0.1 .. `MAX_ZOOM` 2 (moved from `canvas/geometry.ts` to shared; geometry re-exports them), finite only; anything else is a plain `bad_message`. `clampViewportView` rounds x and y to whole units and zoom to 3 decimals.
- Client (strict): `followStart { target }` (a server participant id), `followStop {}`, `viewport { x, y, zoom }`. Server (strict): `viewportUpdate { id, x, y, zoom }` (to that person's followers only; the id is theirs, from their socket), `followersChanged { count }` (to the followed person only, 0 .. `MAX_FOLLOWERS`, never who), `followEnded { reason }` (`FOLLOW_END_REASONS`: `target_left`, `not_found`, `self`). Error `followers_full`. All `BOARD_WRITES: false` (a locked board still follows) and `carriesNoteContent: false`.
- Relay (`room.ts` `follow`, `view`): a socket follows at most one participant; the attachment holds `following` (the target's server id) and nothing else about it, so hibernation needs nothing; follower counts are worked out from the open sockets' attachments (`followersOf`), never kept. Any `followStart` first ends the current follow (the old target hears its new count), then: the same target again = nothing; yourself = `followEnded self`; no joined socket with that id = `followEnded not_found`; `MAX_FOLLOWERS` (10) already = `followers_full` (following nobody); otherwise the target hears `followersChanged`. `followStop` = the target's new count; nothing when following nobody. Before joining: `not_joined`.
- `viewport` is parsed before any rate budget and spends its own per-socket bucket, `VIEWPORT_LIMITS` (10/s, burst 10), never SOCKET_LIMITS or CURSOR_LIMITS. Over budget: dropped and counted as a violation with no reply (20 in 10 s close the socket, as for any abuse). Joined and followed: `viewportUpdate` to each follower through `Room.send` (the per-recipient filter; nothing to filter). No followers, or not joined: dropped, zero fan-out, zero storage calls (tested by wrapping every storage method).
- Clearing (`Room.leave`: close, error, rate-limit eviction): the person it followed hears the lower count, and its own followers get `followEnded target_left` (their attachments cleared), both before `participant_left`. End session: every follower gets `followEnded target_left` before `sessionEnded`. A reconnect is a new socket and a new participant id: it follows nobody and has no followers, and following the old id is `not_found`.
- Why `MAX_FOLLOWERS` 10: each viewport is sent once per follower, so it bounds one message's fan-out (10 x 5 a second); a whole room watching the host is what Bring to me is for (one message, no ongoing fan-out). Below the room size (20), so the cap is reachable and tested.
- Web pure rules (`follow/follow.ts`): `FOLLOW_SEND_INTERVAL_MS` 200 (5 a second), `VIEW_MIN_MOVE` 1, `VIEW_MIN_ZOOM_CHANGE` 0.001; `viewChanged`, `mayShareViewport` (live, at least one follower, never `compact`), `isUserMove` (React Flow's onMoveStart passes an input event for the person's own pan or zoom, none for fit, jump or a follow), `ViewportSender` (leading + one trailing send with the latest view, only when changed; `force` for a new follower; clock and timers injected), `FollowSink`.
- Session (`RoomSession`): `startFollow(id)` (live, someone else here now, never my own ids), `stopFollow()`, `stopOnUserMove()` (the hook part 2 calls from the canvas's user moves), `shareViewport(view, compact)` (the canvas offers every view change; sent only while followed, not hidden, never from a phone, through `ViewportSender`), `bringToMe(view)`. The relay's four messages are handled before the view machinery (never a view update) and go to `options.follow`: `viewportUpdate` only from the person I follow now (a stale one is ignored); `followersChanged` keeps the count and, when it rises, sends my current view at once (a new follower needs it); `followEnded` and the `followers_full` error end my follow; `participant_left` of the person I follow ends it too. Every `joined`, the drop, a resync `snapshot`, `close`, `roomGone` and `finish` forget following both ways (`forgetFollow`: no follow, count 0, the sender reset so a waiting trailing view never goes).
- Store: `follow/followStore.ts` (`useFollow`, `followSink`): `following`, `followers`, `leader` (the followed person's last view), `ended` (why the last follow ended, until the next), `brought` (the last Bring to me, with a `seq` so a repeat is new). Memory only; never touches browser storage (tested).
- Names and ids in these messages are untrusted data: the relay uses its own ids from the sockets and never a client-claimed one (the schemas are strict, so a page can't add `id`, `from` or a name).
- Costs in docs/LIMITS.md "Follow and Bring to me". Tests: `shared/test/follow.test.ts`, `worker/test/follow.test.ts`, `web/test/follow.test.ts`, `web/test/followSession.test.ts`.
- Part 2 (UI) is built: see "Follow and Bring to me: the UI (v0.31.0, part 2 of 2)" below.

## Follow and Bring to me: the UI (v0.31.0, part 2 of 2)
- Web only (no protocol, relay or stored-schema change; protocol 19, schema 10). Bring to me's host control and banner are in facilitation.md "Bring to me: the UI".
- Wiring: `useRoom` passes `follow: followSink` and exposes `startFollow`, `stopFollow`, `stopOnUserMove`, `shareViewport`, `bringToMe`; `roomStore`'s `PublishedRoom` gains `startFollow`, `stopFollow`, `bringToMe` (Participants and the phone Session section are outside the canvas).
- Pure rules and texts: `follow/followUi.ts` (`FOLLOW_TEXT`, `BRING_TEXT`, `followEndText`, `followReason`, `bringReason`, `followDuration`, `MoveGuard`; `BRING_COOLDOWN_MS` 5000, `BROUGHT_BANNER_MS` 60000, `FOLLOW_NOTICE_MS` 5000, `FOLLOW_STREAM_MS` 600). `follow/actions.ts`: `followPerson` (start, then `followStarted(truncateName(name))`), `stopFollowing` (says "Stopped following" only when the session stopped something), `sendBring` (never inside the cooldown, never without a view), and `setViewSource` / `currentView` (the board registers `canvas.view`, so Bring to me works from the Participants sheet).
- Store (`follow/followStore.ts`, still memory only): adds `leaderName` (truncated, from the Follow press), `notice` (one plain line, gone after `FOLLOW_NOTICE_MS`), `announce` (the follow announcer's text: start, stop, end, Bring to me sent; never a viewport), `dismissed` (the banner seq dismissed) and `bringSentAt`. `ended(reason)` sets the notice from `followEndText`; `clear()` empties everything except `bringSentAt` (the cooldown outlives a reconnect).
- Canvas (`canvas/useCanvasView.ts`): `useCanvasView({ onUserMove })`. My own view commands call it first (fit, `fitItems` unless `user` false (the first fit), `zoomTo`, `jumpTo`, `centreAt`, `zoomIn/Out`, `resetZoom`, `reveal`); `recentre` (canvas resize) never does. `follow(view)` and `showView(view)` (Go there) run inside `MoveGuard.programmatic`; `moved(event)` (React Flow `onMove`) stops following only for `guard.userMove(event)` (an input event, outside a programmatic move). `onMove`, not `onMoveStart`: d3 reports a start on a plain pane press, which isn't a pan. The overview map moves with no input event, so `BoardCanvas`'s capture `pointerdown` / `wheel` on `.react-flow__minimap` calls `userGesture()`. Animation: the first follow move takes `VIEW_ANIMATION_MS`, updates within `FOLLOW_STREAM_MS` of the last are set directly, and reduced motion never animates.
- `BoardCanvas` `onMove` also offers every view to `room.shareViewport(viewFromCentre(viewport, size), !multiSelect)`; the session sends only while followed, not hidden, never from a phone, through the 5/s `ViewportSender` (unchanged).
- `RoomBoard`: subscribes to `useFollow` and calls `canvas.follow(leader)` for each new leader view of the person I follow; Stop and Go there call `stopFollowing(room.stopFollow)`; `FollowNotices` sits in the notice stack after the silent strip.
- `follow/FollowNotices.tsx` (in the board's notice stack, so it stacks under the lock banner and the voting and silent strips and never overlaps them or the bottom controls): the always-present polite `data-follow-announcer`; `data-follow-chip` ("Following Sam" or "Waiting for Sam's view." + "Phones and hidden tabs don't share a view.", with `data-follow-stop`, a 44 px **Stop**); `data-followers` "N following you" (md and up only; plain text, not live, a count only); `data-follow-notice`; and the banner (facilitation.md).
- Participants (`rooms/ParticipantsPage.tsx`): `Follow` (`data-follow`, "Follow <name>" / "Stop following <name>", `aria-disabled` + visible "Not connected." reason) before `GoTo` in each other person's row; a Follow press closes the sheet. Rows wrap (`flex-wrap`, the name `flex-auto`) so at 360 the buttons go under a long name or the Host badge instead of squeezing it (found in Chromium). The page doesn't know who is on a phone, so Follow is never off for that; the waiting chip says why nothing arrives.
- Resets: every path that calls `forgetFollow` (joined, drop, resync snapshot, close, roomGone, finish) clears the chip, the notice, the announcer and the banner through `followSink.clear()`.
- Tests: `web/test/followUi.test.ts` (rules, `MoveGuard`, the store's UI part), `web/test/followPage.test.tsx` (the page), help claims in `web/test/help.test.ts`. Checked in headless Chromium against a local relay with a host (1280 light) and two guests (768 light, 360 dark).
