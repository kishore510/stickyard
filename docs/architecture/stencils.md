# Stencils

Architecture notes for stencils. Sections are in build order and keep the version that introduced them; later sections can override earlier ones. A quoted section name may live in another file: see `docs/architecture/README.md`.

## Stencils (v0.39.0, structure part 3 of 3)
- Web only: no protocol, relay or stored-schema change (protocol v22, stored schema 13). A stencil is a small area to drop onto the board: built in, or saved from a selection in this browser. Placing one is the normal add messages, so the relay validates everything again.
- Format (`stencils/data.ts`): the template format (board units, top-left at 0, 0) extended to carry `notes`, `shapes` and `frames` as the add messages' items (`NoteItem`, `ShapeItem` without `rank`, `FrameItem`, each with a `ref`), `arrows` whose ends are `{ ref, anchor }` on a stencil note or shape or a free `{ x, y }`, plus every connector style key, and `groups` `{ label, color, members: refs }`. A stencil has an `id` (built-ins fixed; saved ones random, 12 characters) and a `name` (one line, 40 characters, cleaned like a group label; empty is "Untitled stencil"). `stencilBounds`, `stencilCounts`.
- `parseStencil(raw)` (everything from storage, and the built-ins in a test): exact keys at every level; rects must be finite numbers and are clamped onto the board (`clampNoteRect` etc.); text and titles are cut to their caps and cleaned as the relay would; style and colour keys must be the protocol's (`noteItemSchema`, `shapeItemSchema`, `frameItemSchema`, the connector style schemas); refs must be unique and valid; an arrow end must name a stencil note or shape (never a frame), not both ends the same; a group needs 2 to 100 members, each a stencil note or shape in one group at most; counts within the room caps; at least one item. Anything else: null (the entry is left out and counted). Nothing is ever rendered as HTML: names, labels and text are React text.
- Built-ins (`stencils/builtins.ts`, generic wording): Labelled area (a frame "Topic" with three blank notes), Three-step flow (three rectangles joined by two arrows), Note cluster (four notes in a group "Theme"), Decision (a frame, a diamond, two notes, two arrows), Pros and cons (a frame, two text labels, two groups of two notes).
- Storage (`stencils/storage.ts`): this browser only, `STORAGE_KEYS.stencils` = `stickyard:stencils`, `{ version: 1, stencils: [...] }`. `STENCIL_LIMITS`: 20 stencils, 24 KB each (UTF-8 bytes of its JSON), 200 KB in all. `loadStencils` never throws (blocked or missing storage: `blocked`; a value that isn't JSON, the wrong version, an entry that doesn't parse, a duplicate id or an oversized entry: counted in `malformed`); `saveStencil` (re-parsed, then the caps, each refusal with its reason), `renameStencil`, `deleteStencil`, `deleteAllStencils`. `stencils/stencilStore.ts` (zustand) reads on the board's mount, when the tab opens and after every change; it also holds the Save dialog's open state.
- Session: `RoomSession.placeStencil(stencil, centre)`. Refused up front with the reason (nothing sent): not live, a guest on a locked board (`LOCK_TEXT.reason`), a silent round for everyone (`SILENT_TEXT.on`), another add run or restore or template (`UNDO_TEXT.busy`), and any cap it wouldn't fit, checked in the order frames, notes (`totalNotes`), shapes, arrows (100), groups (30) (`STENCIL_TEXT.noRoom`). Otherwise centred on `centre`, clamped so all of it is on the board, then the Duplicate path: `startItems` (deferred; frames, then notes, then shapes, paced itemsAdd), arrows added locally on the items' local ids and groups likewise (`addArrowToRun`, `addGroupToRun`), one `history.recordAdd("Stencil", ...)` for all of it, and the run's arrows and groups are sent (paced under `CONNECTOR_LIMITS` and `GROUP_LIMITS`) once its items have their ids. One undo deletes everything it added.
- Save as stencil (`buildStencil`): the selected notes, shapes and frames (content, style and text; no id, z, rev or author), refs `n1`, `s1`, `f1` in selection order; arrows that are selected or join two selected items, an end on anything outside the selection freed at its resolved point (`anchorPoint`, as the relay frees ends); groups whose every member is selected; places from the selection's top-left (free arrow points included). From the top bar (Edit from xl; the Arrange panel below xl, beside Order, so the bar keeps one row at 768) and Properties (any selection with notes, shapes or frames). The dialog (`stencils/StencilUi.tsx` `StencilSaveDialog`, a Sheet) says "Includes the text of the selected items. Saved in this browser only." and takes a name of 40 characters.
- Palette (`palette/registry.ts`): the Stencils tab (md and up, never in the phone drawer) has two categories, "Built-in" and "Saved in this browser" (`savedStencilTiles` from `PaletteRoomState.stencils`); `stencilTile` (`data-palette-item` `stencil-<id>` or `saved-<id>`, a miniature preview, `dropSize` = the stencil's bounds), `PaletteActions.placeStencil`, `PaletteContext.stencilReason` (not connected, the lock, the silent round, busy). Under the tiles `StencilManager` (`data-stencil-manager`): the blocked line, the malformed count, each saved stencil with Rename (the browser's prompt) and Delete, Delete all (asked first), and the caps.
- Tests: `web/test/stencils.test.ts` (parse, clamp, refusals including hostile keys, built-ins, storage, caps, blocked, Save as stencil), `stencilsSession.test.ts` (each built-in's counts and order with remapped ids, clamping, one undo, the cap refusals, lock and silent), `stencilsUi.test.tsx` (the tab, saved and hostile names as text, Rename and Delete, malformed, blocked, the dialog), plus `palette.test.ts`, `config.test.ts` (the key) and `help.test.ts`. `npm run shots` shoots the tab, the dialog, a saved stencil and storage blocked at 768 and 1280 in both themes.
- Left out on purpose: stencils on phones, sharing stencils between people or browsers, import or export of stencils.

## The board file reuses the stencil format (v0.40.0)
- Export board file (`export/boardFile.ts`) is `buildStencil` over the whole board, checked by `parseStencil`, without `id` and `name`, plus `format`, `formatVersion` and `stack`. A change to the stencil item schemas changes the board file too: bump `BOARD_FILE_VERSION` when it isn't backwards compatible. Details in canvas.md "Export on phones and the board file".
